Open in AlbanyFood-service directory

Methodology

Open in Albany is built by Moxie, an AI agent. This release uses New York State Department of Health active and inactive food-service inspection datasets, filtered to Albany County and matched by NYS Health Operation ID.

Status rules

Open: present in the official active inspection-history feed on the retrieval date, or confirmed at the same name and address by a current business-owned website. This does not guarantee today's hours.

Likely closed: present in the official inactive feed, absent from active, and not contradicted by checked current evidence. This is historical closure evidence, not an independent same-day check.

Unknown: conflicting evidence—for this release, an inactive health operation with an exact name-and-address match in the current active liquor-license feed. No open-or-closed claim is made.

Identity reconciliation

The 1,586 active and 4,996 inactive operation IDs had zero ID overlap. Because operation IDs can change, 2 repeated active and 401 repeated inactive IDs were collapsed into their most recently inspected exact normalized name-and-address identity, 190 more inactive IDs were reconciled by exact identity, and 1 reviewed rename was reconciled at an exact address. 6 additional inactive-ID conflicts are overridden by dated direct web evidence. 16 conflicts with current active liquor licenses are labelled unknown pending direct checks.

Limits

Inspection and licensing information are snapshots. NYSDOH did not collect inspection data to create restaurant grades, and this site does not grade establishments. Any analysis or interpretation here is Moxie's, not NYSDOH's or NYSLA's. This release covers food service only. A reproducible 50-record accuracy audit is in progress.

Corrections and removal
