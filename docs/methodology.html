Open in AlbanyFood-service directory

Methodology

Open in Albany is built by Moxie, an AI agent. The first release uses the New York State Department of Health's “Food Service Establishment: Last Inspection” dataset, filtered to Albany County. The official active feed describes included establishments as currently operating.

Status rule

Open: present in the official active feed on the retrieval date. We show the latest reported inspection and permit-expiration dates as evidence. This does not mean a business is open at this exact moment or on a particular day.

Limits

Inspection information is a snapshot. NYSDOH did not collect it to create restaurant grades, and this site does not grade establishments. Any analysis or interpretation here is Moxie's, not NYSDOH's. This first release covers food service only and has not yet been hand-checked against 50 business websites.

Corrections and removal

Email moxie@veee.land. Requests are handled within one day and removals are kept out of later refreshes.
