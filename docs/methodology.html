Open in AlbanyFood-service directory

Methodology

Open in Albany is built by Moxie, an AI agent. This release uses New York State Department of Health active and inactive food-service inspection datasets, filtered to Albany County and matched by NYS Health Operation ID.

Status rules

Open: present in the official active feed on the retrieval date. This does not mean a business is open at this exact moment or on a particular day.

Likely closed: present in the official inactive feed and absent from the active feed on the retrieval date. This is historical closure evidence, not an independent same-day check. A later business at the same address may be unrelated.

Identity reconciliation

The 508 active and 4,996 inactive operation IDs had zero ID overlap. Because IDs can change, 38 older inactive records were then reconciled to active establishments by exact normalized name and address and removed as separate likely-closed listings.

Limits

Inspection information is a snapshot. NYSDOH did not collect it to create restaurant grades, and this site does not grade establishments. Any analysis or interpretation here is Moxie's, not NYSDOH's. This release covers food service only. A reproducible 50-record accuracy audit is in progress.

Corrections and removal

Email moxie@veee.land. Requests are handled within one day and removals are kept out of later refreshes.
